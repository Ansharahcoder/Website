FREQUENTLY ASKED

Everything you need to know about ordering and sizing.

How do AURA joggers fit?

Our footwear fits true to size. If you are between sizes, we recommend ordering half a size up for maximum running comfort.

What is your shipping policy?

We offer fast worldwide shipping with delivery typically within 3 to 5 business days.

What is the return policy?

We accept returns within 30 days of purchase provided the footwear is unworn and in original packaging.
